# Niveshak Saathi and the tools people use today

A comparison with 23 tools that Indian investors and fraud victims use today (government, regulators, banks, brokers and private apps), the problems they share, and what Niveshak Saathi does about each. Researched on 3 October 2026 from official pages, press releases and Google Play listings (India store). App-store figures and features change often; anything we could not confirm is marked "not verified". Claims about other products are paraphrased, each with its source.

## Problems the market shares, and what Niveshak Saathi does

| Problem | Where we saw it | Niveshak Saathi |
|---|---|---|
| **Too many doors, and the wrong door says no.** About ten portals, each with its own login and exclusions; SCORES does not take unregistered tip groups or forged documents | SCORES FAQ Q3, Q4, Q39; Chakshu is not for money already lost; RBI Ombudsman FAQ 16 | One "Where to complain" router across SEBI, RBI, IRDAI, PFRDA, IEPF and police/1930, with a "Not sure" route that never guesses; the SCORES routes say what SCORES will not take and where to go instead |
| **Sign-up, KYC and OTP walls** | SCORES (PAN, OTP), cybercrime portal (Indian mobile OTP), SMART ODR, Scamio, Truecaller | No account or sign-up; the complaint packet prepares the facts before a person files |
| **English first; no Hinglish** | Google Messages scam detection lists no Hindi ([Pixel Help](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/16704479?hl=en)); AI UPI Help launched in English; SEBI's "Spot a Scam" is in English | Hindi first, English one tap away; the checker reads Hindi, Hinglish and English; other languages get an "outside coverage" note and the official helplines that speak them |
| **Private messages leave the phone; ads** | Scamio, ScamDekho upload content; Truecaller shows ads; Trend Micro asks for SMS and call logs | The check runs on the phone; nothing is saved or sent; no ads, analytics or cloud AI |
| **Little help after the money is gone** | Truecaller's insurance only for Premium users; Norton reimburses US residents only; awareness tools stop at advice | "Get help now" puts the bank and 1930 first, then evidence, no "recovery fee", the right complaint route and a complaint packet |
| **General scam tools miss investment tricks** | Call-focused or bank-focused tools; investment schemes were 77% of 2025 cyber-fraud losses, as IANS reports from I4C data (second-hand figure) | Investment-specific signs: guaranteed returns, tip groups, fake IPO allotment, paid recovery agents, withdrawal fees, borrowing to trade, impersonation, "digital arrest" |
| **Official checks are scattered and misread** | People read SEBI Check's rejection of a personal UPI ID as a fault in their own ID (Saa₹thi Play reviews) | "Before you pay" links SEBI Check, "@valid" UPI IDs, ASBA for IPOs, RBI Sachet and I4C's Suspect Search (with I4C's warning that "not found" proves nothing), and never calls a payee safe |
| **Text-heavy, form-heavy screens** | Long English dropdowns and FAQs (SEBI "Spot a Scam", SCORES, IEPF) | Large picture tiles, black-and-white line drawings, chips instead of long lists, read-aloud and large text |
| **App installs and new-phone requirements** | Sanchar Saathi, Saa₹thi, Truecaller, Trend Micro need installs; Google's newest models need recent phones | One web page, about 242 KB to download; works offline once saved; shareable as a file |
| **No pause at the moment of pressure** | Google adds a 30-second pause during risky calls; McAfee's India survey says a typical scam unfolds in 30 minutes or less (vendor research) | "Before you pay" says STOP or VERIFY before any money moves, and a STOP or High-risk answer offers a 30-second pause |

## Ideas we are borrowing

From the market's best patterns (sources in the research notes):
- **Colour plus symbol plus word for every result** (NPCI's UPI Help): our verdicts pair a drawing, a word and a colour, so they read without colour.
- **"If money is already gone, go here instead"** (Chakshu): the 1930 and bank route stays one tap away on every prevention screen.
- **"Official help is free" and "no agent is needed"** (Bima Bharosa, IEPF): already in the IEPF guide and the "no recovery fee" step.
- **Topic tiles and pictures** (RBI Kehta Hai, RBI's pictorial booklets): the redesigned Home and page drawings.
- **Honest limits on look-ups** (I4C says its suspect database is incomplete): matches our rule that "no known signs" is never "safe".

## Where others are ahead

- **Reach:** Truecaller (over 100 crore downloads) and Sanchar Saathi (over 1 crore) dwarf any new app.
- **Real-time protection** inside calls, messages and payments (Google, Truecaller, UPI apps).
- **Real powers:** freezing money through 1930, Ombudsman awards and broker freezes; Niveshak Saathi can only point to them.
- **In-person help:** IEPFA's Niveshak Shivir and Niveshak Didi camps.

## Added to Niveshak Saathi on 3 October 2026

Each piece of advice cites the official page it rests on, checked on 3 October 2026; the two new sources show "review pending" until the named reviewer confirms them.
1. **"Not this door"** on the three SCORES routes (broker, company or registrar, mutual fund): SCORES does not take complaints about unregistered or unregulated activity, fake or forged documents, or other regulators' matters ([SCORES FAQ](https://scores.sebi.gov.in/faqs), Q3), so the note points to "Possible fraud" instead.
2. **"Freeze it"** in Get help now: if a trading app login, password or OTP was shared, ask the broker to freeze online access to the trading account, which every broker must offer from 1 July 2024, and the DP to freeze the demat account ([SEBI circular, 12 January 2024](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/jan-2024/ease-of-doing-investments-by-investors-facility-of-voluntary-freezing-blocking-of-trading-accounts-by-clients_80597.html)).
3. **I4C's Suspect Search** in Before you pay, when paying a UPI ID or a person's account, with I4C's own warning that its database is not complete, so "not found" proves nothing ([Suspect Search](https://cybercrime.gov.in/Webform/suspect_search_repository.aspx)).
4. **"Pause for 30 seconds"** after a STOP or High-risk answer: a calm screen with a ring that empties over 30 seconds, like the pause some phones now add during risky calls. It is labelled a general safety step.
