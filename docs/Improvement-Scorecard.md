# Niveshak Saathi against the judging criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Delivered | Evidence still needed |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Resilience and safety** | 30% | Before-payment STOP/VERIFY checks; message-risk and reliability explanation; emergency steps with bank and 1930 first; official grievance routes; privacy-safe Action Packet; family readiness; practice; family warning; three persona safety plans | Consented field pilot showing comprehension, completed actions and avoided loss |
| **Bharat-first usability** | 25% | Hindi-first with English and Roman-Hindi understanding; large task choices; Praveen, Kavita and Babulal plans; read-aloud; 320-pixel fit; large text; offline use; no profile or account setup | Native Hindi review and tests with Tier-2/3 users on physical low-end phones |
| **Guardrails and trust** | 15% | No tips, ads or monetisation; no cloud inference, analytics, account connection or storage; official sources with review dates; explicit uncertainty and outside-coverage abstention | Independent security review and an accountable ongoing source-review process |
| **Technical execution** | 15% | Explainable on-device hybrid: multilingual signals, fitted logistic weights, safety floors and five reliability states; own-words assistant; 1,229 automated checks; three sealed synthetic blind sets | Representative consented real-message evaluation and drift monitoring |
| **Feasibility and scale** | 15% | Static cacheable app; no per-check server/inference cost; 151,758-byte gzip build; owner-controlled content Studio and manifests; pilot export; staged national architecture plan | CDN load tests, operational monitoring, language rollout and named distribution partners |

**Final blind result:** 72.4% of fraud or suspicious messages warned, 71.9% of fraud at High, 8.6% of ordinary messages warned, and 7.0% of ordinary messages at High. The full intervals and limits are in `Validation-v3.md`.

The current prototype has strong evidence for a hackathon demonstration. A perfect judging score cannot be guaranteed, and the project does not claim that 16 crore accounts have been load-tested or that user harm has already been reduced. The scale design and rollout gates are in `SCALE-AND-RELIABILITY.md`.
