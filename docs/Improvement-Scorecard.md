# Release 3.2 against the judging criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Delivered in Release 3.2 | Remaining external evidence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Resilience and safety | 30% | **Before you pay:** STOP/VERIFY with SEBI's "@valid" UPI rule, SEBI Check, IPO-by-ASBA and RBI Sachet.<br>**Message check:** a model v3.2 risk verdict.<br>**Emergency mode:** own-words input, bank and 1930 first.<br>**Navigator:** official time limits.<br>**Action Packet:** a privacy gate.<br>**Family readiness:** SEBI's 2026 nomination rules.<br>**Practice:** before and after scores.<br>**"Warn my family".**<br>**Pilot session:** a built-in facilitator mode, so a real pilot can run in an evening | Real-user outcomes: the pilot is ready but has not yet been run |
| Bharat-first | 25% | Hindi-first everywhere with English; Roman-Hindi understanding.<br>A calm, simple layout: Home asks one question with six large choices; a sidebar menu groups the tools under three plain headings (a labelled Menu button on phones); "Get urgent help" is always in the top bar; less-used tools sit under More tools; simple line icons, no emoji.<br>Every page fits 320 px phones at normal, large and 200% text, in both languages.<br>Own words typed or spoken through the phone keyboard's microphone; help to install a Hindi voice.<br>"Share this app" over WhatsApp (the link, or the offline file).<br>120 Hindi fixes from an independent review.<br>One offline file of about 138 KB compressed | Native-speaker review; physical low-end phones; target-user testing |
| Trust | 15% | No commerce and no tips; tips are flagged as a warning sign.<br>Every step cites an official page with its review date and named reviewer (Himanshi Rathore, 31 enabled sources).<br>Three independent reviews fixed; no uploads or storage.<br>The checker shows how it decided, and its measured miss rate is published | Accountable ongoing review; independent security assessment |
| Technology | 15% | An explainable on-device risk model.<br>Its High rule is simple: a safety floor, two different signs, or one strong sign.<br>Return maths and link analysis; an own-words assistant.<br>**Three sealed blind sets,** each used once for the version it tested.<br>About 1 ms per message; more than 1,100 automated checks | Real-message corpus; screen-reader and wider device coverage |
| Feasibility | 15% | No server or inference cost.<br>The Owner Studio governs all content with validation and manifests.<br>Sources are reviewed by a named owner.<br>The pilot session exports anonymous results with a live summary.<br>"Share this app"; a publishing checklist; an embeddable engine | Partners; a completed pilot; publishing v3.2 with a public link |

**Final blind result** (`Blind-Evaluation.json`, third sealed set, scored once after v3.2 was frozen):

| | v3.2 | v3.1 | v2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fraud or suspicious messages warned | 72.4% | 68.2% | 35.4% |
| Fraud at High | 71.9% | 70.3% | 13.3% |
| Ordinary messages warned | 8.6% | 14.1% | 24.2% |
| Ordinary messages at High | 7.0% | 11.7% | 2.3% |

v3.2 catches more than twice as many scams as v2, and raises far fewer false alarms overall. Ordinary messages at High remain above v2's rate. They all fall among the deliberately tricky look-alikes; this is the main open weakness.

A defensible top score still needs evidence from consenting users, independent reviewers and real devices. No score is guaranteed, and no impact result has been invented.
