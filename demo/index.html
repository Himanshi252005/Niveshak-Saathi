Niveshak Saathi: demo video

निवेशक साथी का डेमो वीडियो · Release 3.5 · about 4–5 minutes, with English and Hindi captions on screen.

What it shows: Kavita checks a fake IPO offer and "Before you pay" says stop; what to do if she had already paid; the bank complaint route up to the RBI Ombudsman; Babulal's old shares and the IEPF-5 guide, then his family asset map; Praveen's F&O tip, in English; the app working offline.

Recorded from the real app (the same build as the live link) in a desktop browser that imitates a phone. Every person, message, phone number and UPI ID is made up. Nothing was called, paid or submitted.
