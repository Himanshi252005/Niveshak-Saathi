Niveshak Saathi: demo video

निवेशक साथी का डेमो वीडियो · Release 3.5 · about 4–5 minutes, with English and Hindi captions on screen.

What it shows: Kavita checks a fake IPO offer, and "Before you pay" says stop and offers a 30-second pause; what to do if she had already paid, including freezing online access to her trading account; the bank complaint route up to the RBI Ombudsman; Babulal's old shares and the IEPF-5 guide, then his family asset map; Praveen's F&O tip, in English; the app working offline; the mint menu and the dark theme.

Recorded from the real app on 3 October 2026, before the menu turned emerald and light became the default, in a desktop browser that imitates a phone. Every person, message, phone number and UPI ID is made up. Nothing was called, paid or submitted.
